Questions

Do I need a bank account?

No. All you need is a phone number. Your Fakka wallet holds your balance.

Is there a minimum or maximum?

Fakka is designed for small change, from 25 piasters up to EGP 20 per transaction.

Can I cash out?

Yes. Once your balance reaches EGP 50 you can move it to a mobile wallet or bank account.

What does it cost merchants?

Merchants top up a change float. Pricing will be announced at launch.
